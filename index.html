01 / Research

From training data to predictions.

Interpretability through a kernel lens.

Manuscript under review at TMLR

A Tale of Two Readouts: Unmasking Data Attribution through a Unified Kernel Framework

NTK-JL uses a regularised kernel surrogate built from a trained network’s Jacobian features to study two distinct attribution questions: how training examples contribute to a fitted prediction, and how predictions respond to changes in the training data.

The framework makes the connections to Representer Point Selection and single-model TRAK explicit under matched assumptions. Parameter subsampling and Johnson–Lindenstrauss sketching make the retained gradient representation more compact.
